Microsoft Word: Build Better Documents

Six short lessons on building reports that behave: start from the approved template, use styles, control sections and page numbers, fix tables and images, and share an accessible PDF before the 3 pm deadline.

Challenge & context

A report passes through five or more people before it is sent, and one bad habit early on breaks it for everyone after: contents pages that miss sections, page numbers that restart, blank pages that will not delete.

Instructional strategy

Every lesson works on the same practice report, which has real formatting faults. Content is revealed with Continue, some activities must be finished first, each lesson ends with a knowledge check, and lessons unlock in order.

Content & governance

Builds the habits that keep shared documents reliable: the approved template, heading styles, automatic contents, section breaks, version history on OneDrive or SharePoint, and a tagged, bookmarked PDF that works with screen readers.

Interactive features

A labelled graphic of hidden formatting marks, flashcards, sorting, step-by-step processes, tabs, matching and multi-response checks, and a branching scenario with Sam Rivera. AI voice-over with transcript and speed control in every lesson.
