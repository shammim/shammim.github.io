1. Project summary

A one-page course blueprint and assessment model for a blended dispatcher pathway on Open LMS (Moodle), delivered as an interactive explainer module.

Part 1 orients dispatch trainees to their pathway: what each section asks of them, how they are assessed and what earns the certificate. Part 2 opens the design for L&D and operations leads: the needs analysis, the Moodle build and the pre/post evaluation model that shows whether learning worked.

2. Analysis

AreaFinding
Learning needs analysisCAD call audit logs, QA breach reports and supervisor feedback across dispatch centres, triangulated to confirm the behavioural friction point.
Failure pointMisclassifying high-priority triage codes, and escalating caller panic under shift pressure.
LearnersEmergency call-takers and dispatch trainees: high cognitive load, auditory fatigue, rostered shifts.
EnvironmentMulti-monitor CAD terminals; learning delivered between shifts through Open LMS and its mobile app.
Behaviour targetCode the right priority, fast, while the caller is panicking.
Business measuresBaseline compliance and QA benchmarks, compared with post-training and 30-day observation results.

3. Design approach

DecisionChoiceWhy
ModalityBlended pathway in Open LMS: micro-learning SCORM, peer forum, live debrief, quizzes and a supervisor checklistMoodle gives native completion tracking, gradebook integration and progression gates.
Progression gatesActivity completion and access restrictionsTrainees cannot reach live CAD calls until they have mastered the triage steps.
Same scale, before and afterBaseline and post-assessment use matching scenarios and confidence itemsMakes the pre/post comparison meaningful.
ADDIE with SAM loopsADDIE for governance and sign-off; rapid SAM iterations for the scenario buildKeeps stakeholders aligned while the build moves quickly.
AccessibilityWCAG 2.1 AAPlain English, high contrast, keyboard operable.

4. Learning objectives

By the end, the learner can:

  • Describe the six sections of the pathway and what each one asks of you.
  • Complete a baseline scenario and confidence check before the core scenario.
  • State the three conditions that earn the certificate.
  • Explain (for L&D and operations leads) how the Moodle build and the Kirkpatrick Level 1–3 model show whether learning worked.
