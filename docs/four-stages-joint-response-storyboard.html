1. Project summary

A performance support package for multi-agency dispatch. A one-page job aid turns joint escalation and inter-agency handoff procedures into four visual stages: triage and identify need, select agencies, execute the handoff, joint log and verify.

Dispatchers practise with the aid on a two-minute highway collision that needs police, fire and ambulance, so the first time they use it under pressure is not on shift. Part 2 of the module shows the design decisions for operations and L&D leads.

2. Analysis

AreaFinding
Learning needs analysisMulti-agency incident logs, joint dispatch friction reports and supervisor audit debriefs across police, fire and ambulance dispatch.
Break-pointsDelayed handoffs (agencies alerted one after another), incorrect escalation coding (entrapment or fuel leaks not treated as multi-agency) and duplicate data entry for each agency.
LearnersCall-takers, dispatchers and multi-agency coordinators: high cognitive load and time scarcity.
Where the aid livesBeside a dual-monitor CAD station, as an A4 desk laminate under shift lighting, and as a tagged PDF on a tablet.
Behaviour targetSelect every agency the triggers need at once, send one METHANE report and verify the joint log.
Business measuresInter-agency handoff time, handoff error rate and supervisor ratings, before rollout and at 30 days.

3. Design approach

DecisionChoiceWhy
Performance supportBuilt for the “moment of apply” (Gottfredson & Mosher)The aid is one click away inside the scenario and on the desk at the CAD station.
Cognitive load & Mayer’s coherenceFour stages, one action per line, no decorative imagesEverything on the page earns its place.
Colour with a backupPolice, fire and ambulance colours always paired with an icon and a labelReadable with colour blindness, in print and in greyscale.
Script anchorsOne line to say per stage, in quotesGives dispatchers words under pressure.
METHANE stripAcross the foot of the pageAlways visible during Stage 3 without turning the page.
AccessibilityWCAG 2.1 AA; Lato headings, Inter body, 9 pt minimum in printLegible on an A4 laminate at arm’s length.

4. Learning objectives

By the end, the learner can:

  • Use the four-stage job aid to identify multi-agency triggers on a live call.
  • Select every agency the triggers require, not just the first one heard.
  • Build a METHANE report and patch the shared talkgroup within the time limit.
  • Verify acknowledgements and link the joint CAD log.
