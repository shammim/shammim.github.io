ISBAR Clinical Handover - Quick Reference Job Aid

Aligned to NSQHS Standard 6: Communicating for Safety | Point-of-Care Companion

Component Key Prompts & Questions to Answer Good Practice & Nursing Example
I - Introduction • Who am I, and what is my role?
• Where am I calling from (ward/bed)?
• Who is the patient? (Full Name, DOB, URN/MRN)		 • Good Practice: Use 3 approved identifiers every time; check wristband.
• Example: "Hi, I'm Sam Patel, RN on Ward 6B. I'm calling about Margaret Hollis, DOB 14/03/1954, URN 448219, in bed 12."
S - Situation • What is happening right now?
• Why am I calling or handing over?
• How worried am I?		 • Good Practice: Keep to 1-2 sentences. Lead with the problem, state level of concern early.
• Example: "I'm worried she's deteriorating. Over the last hour she has become increasingly short of breath and is febrile."
B - Background • Admission diagnosis & procedure?
• Relevant medical history/treatment?
• Allergies or clinical alerts?
• What is normal/baseline for this patient?		 • Good Practice: Share relevant context, not everything. Always include allergies.
• Example: "Day 1 post laparoscopic right hemicolectomy. Has COPD and penicillin allergy (rash). Baseline SpO2 room air is 94%."
A - Assessment • Latest vital signs & Early Warning Score (NEWS2/ADDS)?
• What has changed over time?
• Physical assessment findings?
• Clinical impression?		 • Good Practice: Give numbers, not adjectives. Describe trends over time.
• Example: "RR 24, SpO2 93% on 2L, HR 112, BP 102/68, T 38.3. NEWS2 is 10. Right base crackles, urine output 20mL/hr for 2 hrs."
R - Recommendation • What do I want to happen, and by when?
• What should be done in the meantime?
• What tasks are pending?
• Have we confirmed the plan?		 • Good Practice: Be specific (what, who, when). Use closed-loop communication (read-back).
• Example: "I need a doctor review within 15 mins. Should I collect blood cultures/lactate? Please read back the plan."

CRITICAL ESCALATION & CLOSED-LOOP PROTOCOL:

1. Closed-Loop Read-Back: Always request or deliver a verbal read-back of all order details and clinical management plans.
2. Escalation Authority: If a medical response does not match the clinical risk level or score (e.g., NEWS2 ≥ 7), invoke local escalation policies (e.g., Rapid Response / MET Call) immediately.
