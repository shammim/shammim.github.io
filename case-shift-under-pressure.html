Shift Under Pressure: Cyber Awareness Simulation

Sector & audienceLocal government · council staff who handle residents’ personal information

Target behaviour changeReport suspicious emails and lock the screen every time you step away, even when the queue is long.

Ecosystem deliverables
  • 7-minute simulation pilot with three endings
  • Design document with CCAF tables, a scenario map and a roadmap
  • Non-training fixes handed to IT: Report button, auto-lock and individual logins

My roleLearning experience designer and developer: performance analysis, action mapping, CCAF scenario design, design document, 3D simulation build, accessibility and evaluation plan.

Start the courseAll case studies

Engaging learning experience

  • A shift clock, three meters and residents served out of seven
  • Delayed consequences: a link opened at 9:40 comes back at 11:40
  • Three endings: Safe shift, Close call or Incident shift

Technical stack

  • Custom HTML5, CSS and JavaScript course build
  • Three.js 3D scene
  • Variables for the clock, three meters and delayed consequences
  • Design document with CCAF tables and a scenario map

Accessibility

  • WCAG 2.2 AA design: every action has a button
  • The clock moves only when the learner acts; a text description of each view; reduced motion respected
  • Automated WCAG 2.2 AA check (axe-core) on the opening screen: no issues found

At a glance

Audience
Council staff who handle residents’ personal information: customer service, rates, libraries and depots
Format
Animated 3D simulation of a council customer service centre, built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Three.js, plus a design document
Structure
Pilot: one shift with 2 decision points and 3 endings, about 7 minutes
Assessment
Shift outcome, warning signs found, and minutes spent on safety compared with minutes lost to incidents; Kirkpatrick plan using phishing report rates and screen-lock spot checks
Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA: every action has a button, the clock moves only when the learner acts, a text description of each view, reduced motion respected

Highlights

  • Animation

    An animated 3D council customer service centre, with residents queuing at the counter.

  • Design system

    Built with my SS Learning Design system (AI-assisted, created with Claude): its colour tokens, type and accessible controls, applied to a 3D scene.
