About me

FAQ

Questions clients ask

How do you work with our subject matter experts?

I keep their time short and focused: one kick-off conversation, then two review rounds at set points, design sign-off and the near-final build. I bring questions and drafts, so they react to something rather than start from a blank page.

What do you need from us?

Time from one subject matter expert, your existing content and policies, your LMS details, and one person who can sign things off.

What will we get at the end?

Whatever we agree in the quote. Usually that’s the SCORM package and source files, the facilitator, learner and manager resources, and a narrated walkthrough video of the finished course.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. After the discovery call, you’ll get a proposal with a timeline and milestones.

Can you work with our template, brand or authoring tool?

Yes. I build in Articulate 360 and in custom HTML, CSS and JavaScript templates, and I can match your brand and style guide.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I’m based in Melbourne and work remotely, so I can work with teams anywhere. Calls, reviews and hand-over all happen online.
