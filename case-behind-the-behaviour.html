Behind the Behaviour

Sector & audienceEarly childhood education and care in Victoria · Cert III and Diploma educators and graduate teachers

Target behaviour changeDescribe what you see and respond to the need behind a behaviour, then talk with families without blame.

Ecosystem deliverables
  • 2.5-hour SCORM course with role pathways
  • ABC Observation Sheet
  • Family Conversation Guide

My roleLearning experience designer and developer: audience analysis, objectives mapped to the VEYLDF and NQS, three branching and three animated scenarios, voice-over script, build, job aids and accessibility testing.

Start the courseAll case studies

Engaging learning experience

  • A trust meter that rises and falls in the parent conversation
  • A Needs Detective activity where you gather clues
  • A capstone that follows one child for a month

Technical stack

  • Custom HTML5, CSS and JavaScript course build
  • SCORM 1.2 and 2004 reporting
  • Role-based pathways
  • Animated vector scenes
  • AI neural voice-over
  • ABC Observation Sheet and Family Conversation Guide

Accessibility

  • WCAG 2.2 AA design: full keyboard operation, nothing autoplays
  • Voice-over and transcript on every screen, captions and audio description
  • Keyboard and accessibility testing

At a glance

Audience
Cert III and Diploma educators and graduate early childhood teachers in long day care and kindergarten, Victoria
Format
Rise-style HTML course with role pathways, SCORM 1.2 and SCORM 2004 reporting
Structure
Welcome, six modules, a capstone and a 10-question final check (80% pass), about 2.5 hours
Standards
Updated VEYLDF · EYLF V2.0 · NQS QA1, QA2, QA5, QA6 · Child Safe Standards
Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA: voice-over and transcript on every screen, nothing autoplays, full keyboard operation, captions and audio description

Highlights

  • Animation

    Three animated vector scenarios put real room moments on screen, with captions and audio description.

  • Design system

    Built with my SS Learning Design system (AI-assisted, created with Claude): Inter and Lato type, colour tokens and the Rise-style course player.
