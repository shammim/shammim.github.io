Behind the Behaviour

A Rise-style course that teaches new educators and graduate teachers to read behaviour as communication, find the need underneath it and partner with families without blame, with three animated AI scenarios and a capstone that follows one child for a month.

Challenge & context

New educators meet biting, clinging, refusing and worried families in their first months. Without a way to read behaviour, responses default to “stop it”, observations fill with labels, and family conversations slip into blame.

Instructional strategy

Every module starts from a real moment in a toddler or kinder room, not from theory. Learners pick a role: educators focus on the in-the-moment response, and graduate teachers also get a “Leading the response” panel in every module. A capstone follows one child, Arjun, from first observation to a family conversation and a two-week review.

Content & compliance

Mapped to the updated VEYLDF and EYLF V2.0, and to NQS Quality Areas 1, 2, 5 and 6. Covers Victoria’s Child Safe Standards, mandatory reporting, the Preschool Field Officer service and Kindergarten Inclusion Support. Confidence ratings before and after, a reaction survey and an 80% final check cover Kirkpatrick Levels 1 and 2.

Interactive features

An iceberg hotspot graphic, flip cards, two sorting activities (expected or worth a closer look; sort the ABC), a needs detective, step-by-step processes, three branching scenarios including a parent conversation with a trust meter, three animated AI scenarios with a voice per character, and a reflective journal and action plan learners can print.
