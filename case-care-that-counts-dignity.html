Care That Counts: Dignity and Choice

A 10-minute micro-lesson that gives care workers four habits for respectful personal care, then has them practise respecting Mrs Okafor’s choice when the shift is under pressure.

Challenge & context

On a busy shift, personal care gets rushed. A person’s choice quietly becomes the worker’s convenience, and privacy slips in corridors and bathrooms.

Instructional strategy

One short scrolling lesson: why it matters, four habits, a dignified shower in five steps, a sorting activity, a branching conversation with Mrs Okafor, a scored quiz and a next-shift takeaway.

Content & compliance

Built on the Aged Care Quality Standards and the Statement of Rights. The LXP receives completion, an 80% pass mark, the score and every quiz and scenario response, with bookmarking so learners resume where they left off.

Interactive features

Accordion, step-by-step, sorting, a two-turn branching chat with scored replies and feedback, a five-question quiz and a Continue gate. AI voice-over on every section with synced captions and speed control. No outside requests, so it runs behind firewalls.

Screen-by-screen storyboard

Storyboard frames for each screen: what’s on screen, how the learner interacts and what they hear. Select a screen to see it full size.

CTC-01Hero
Hero

Dignity and choice in personal care

On screen
Care That Counts · About 10 minutes. A care worker and a resident share a cup of tea by a window.
Interaction
Lesson menu, progress bar and Start. Keyboard shortcuts: M menu, N next, P previous, K shortcut list.
Audio
AI voice-over (British English) with synced captions, speed control and transcript.
CTC-03Accordion
Accordion

Four habits

On screen
Knock and ask · Offer a real choice · Protect privacy · Go at their pace. Each panel ends with a line to say, such as “Would you like your shower now, or after lunch?”
Interaction
Four panels. The Continue gate opens when all four have been read.
Audio
Voice-over reads each panel as it opens.
CTC-04Step-by-step
Step-by-step

A dignified shower in five steps

On screen
Introduce yourself · Ask permission and offer choices · Prepare so the person is never exposed · Talk through each step · Ask how it was.
Interaction
Numbered steps with Previous and Next. A resident’s room illustration with the call button within reach.
Audio
Voice-over with captions.
CTC-05Sorting
Sorting

Respectful or not?

On screen
Six actions, such as “Choosing the clothes yourself because it is quicker”, sorted into Protects dignity or Puts dignity at risk.
Interaction
Sort by keyboard or touch. Each item shows a one-line reason once placed.
Audio
Voice-over for the instructions.
CTC-06Branching chat
Branching chat

Try it: Mrs Okafor

On screen
“I’d rather have my shower after lunch, dear. I’m not ready yet.” Then a colleague whispers, “Don’t make a fuss. Nobody checks this stuff.”
Interaction
Two turns, three replies each, scored 2, 1 or 0 with feedback after every reply. Each reply is sent to the LXP as an interaction.
Audio
Voice-over with captions.
CTC-07Quiz
Quiz

Check yourself

On screen
Five questions, from “Which of these is a real choice?” to what to do when a colleague says to hurry.
Interaction
Radio options with a rationale after each answer. Pass at 80% (4 of 5). Retakes allowed; the latest result counts.
Audio
Voice-over reads each question.
CTC-08Takeaway
Takeaway

Your next-shift takeaway

On screen
Pick one habit and use it with every person you support today. Then tell your buddy how it went.
Interaction
Completion is sent to the LXP. Session time reported on exit.
Audio
Voice-over with captions.
Job aid · A4

Dignity and Choice Card

The four habits with lines to say, the dignified shower in five steps, what to do when a person says “not now”, and the never list.
