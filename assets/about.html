About me

Learning design that changes what people do

I’m Shammim, a learning designer & developer based in Melbourne, VIC. I turn business needs and dense standards into short, practical learning that people can use on their next shift, and I build the courses myself.

See my workView resume

My background

Computer Science, teaching, and the space between

My background is in Computer Science and teaching. That mix means I can talk to developers and LMS administrators about SCORM, xAPI and accessibility, and to educators and subject matter experts about what a learner actually needs to do differently.

I design for regulated, high-stakes work: early childhood education and care, aged care, NDIS support, clinical handover and field compliance. I also build practical software courses on real workplace data.

I build in Articulate 360 and in hand-coded HTML, CSS and JavaScript course templates, so each course in a series shares one accessible interface.

My approach

What makes me different

Design for real-world application

I go beyond delivering information. I create experiences that help learners understand, practise and apply new skills in authentic workplace situations.

Bridge technology and learning

With a background in Computer Science and teaching, I understand both the technology behind digital platforms and the principles that make learning work.

Think like a learner

I always ask: who are the learners, what do they already know, and what needs to change? That keeps every module relevant, clear and purposeful.

Structure with creativity

I combine instructional design frameworks, UX thinking, visual communication and authoring tools to build clean, intuitive, interactive modules.

Design, test, reflect and iterate

Instructional design is iterative. I use feedback, assessment evidence and analytics to keep refining learning outcomes.

An educator’s perspective

Classroom and workshop experience taught me that learners need varied pacing, scaffolding and active engagement to succeed.
