Sector & audienceEarly childhood education and care · room leaders and educators who publish learning stories

Target behaviour changePublish learning stories that use strengths-based language, the right EYLF outcomes and only children with consent.

Ecosystem deliverables

  • On-shift documentation cheat sheet

My roleLearning experience designer and developer: objectives, the flawed draft, the software simulation, validation rules, build, cheat sheet and testing.

Engaging learning experience

Technical stack

  • Custom HTML5, CSS and JavaScript course build
