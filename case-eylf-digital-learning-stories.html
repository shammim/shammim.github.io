EYLF 2.0 Digital Learning Stories

A software simulation where educators fix a flawed learning story in a mock portfolio app: rewrite deficit language, blur a non-consented child and tag the right EYLF outcomes.

Challenge & context

Learning stories are evidence of the assessment and planning cycle and also records about a child. Every published story has to meet good pedagogy and lawful handling of children’s information at the same time.

Instructional strategy

A 30-minute scaffolded software simulation. Learners first spot what’s wrong with a draft, then fix it unguided in "RoomJournal", a simulated portfolio platform.

Content & compliance

Aligned to NQS Quality Area 1, EYLF V2.0 Outcome 4, Child Safe Principle 8, the Privacy Act 1988 (APP 6 and 11) and National Regulations 181–184.

Interactive features

Text validation against six learning indicators, redaction tools for a background child, EYLF tagging and a privacy breach layer with the steps to take.

"Leo was naughty and pushed SAM when the tower fell down…" with EYLF 1.1 and 5.1 ticked.

EY-L3-01Software simulation

Test Me: fix and publish the story

On screen
Three tasks: 1 Narrative, 2 Media, 3 Learning links. Then Validate & publish.
Interaction
Text editor checked for six indicators, redaction zone tool (crop or blur), EYLF outcome picker. Variables: Text_Valid, Redact_Valid, Tag_Valid, Privacy_Breach, Attempts.
Audio
None. Validation runs only on publish, as it would on shift.
