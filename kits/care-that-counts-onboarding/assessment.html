Part A · Multiple choice

Choose the best answer.

1. A resident would rather shower after lunch, but the roster says morning. What do you do?
  1. Shower them in the morning anyway
  2. Respect their preference and talk to your team leader about the roster
  3. Skip the shower
  4. Ask the family to decide
2. Mr Patel is at the door with his coat on, saying he needs to go home. What is the best response?
  1. Tell him he lives here now
  2. Lock the door and walk away
  3. Acknowledge the feeling, stay with him and gently redirect
  4. Call the family immediately
3. Which scheme covers reporting serious incidents in residential aged care?
  1. NDIS Quality and Safeguards
  2. Serious Incident Response Scheme (SIRS)
  3. WorkCover
  4. Fair Work
4. At a meal, a resident on texture-modified food is given a regular sandwich. What do you do?
  1. Let them eat it slowly
  2. Stop, check the diet card and get the correct meal
  3. Cut it into small pieces
  4. Ask another resident to watch them
5. What does the Code of Conduct for Aged Care require?
  1. Only that you follow the roster
  2. That you act with respect, support choice, and report concerns
  3. That you never talk to families
  4. That you avoid all physical contact

Part B · Short answer

6. Describe two ways you can offer choice during personal care.
7. Explain what you would do if you noticed a sudden change in a resident’s condition.

Part C · Scenario

What would you do? Explain your actions and why.

Marking guide (for assessors)

Part A answers

  1. B. Respect their preference and talk to your team leader about the roster Choice is a right. Raise the roster issue rather than override the person.
  2. C. Acknowledge the feeling, stay with him and gently redirect Validate the emotion, then redirect calmly.
  3. B. Serious Incident Response Scheme (SIRS) SIRS covers the identification, management and reporting of serious incidents.
  4. B. Stop, check the diet card and get the correct meal Always check the diet card. The wrong texture is a choking risk.
  5. B. That you act with respect, support choice, and report concerns The Code sets standards of behaviour, including respect, choice, safety and speaking up.

Part B model answers

  1. Ask before starting; offer options (time, clothes, order of tasks); check in as you go; accept no.
  2. Stay with them, keep them safe, tell the nurse or team leader straight away using ISBAR, and record it.

Part C: look for

  • Keeps the resident safe and calm
  • Reports to the nurse or team leader straight away
  • Records facts accurately
  • Follows the incident and SIRS procedure

Result: all Part D criteria observed, and Parts A to C answered to the standard above. Where something is not yet shown, give feedback and agree a time to reassess.
