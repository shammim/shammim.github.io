Facilitator guide

Digital Skills for Seniors: getting online with confidence
Older Australians who are new to computers, through a library, community centre or seniors group

SessionWeekly drop-in: practise with a friendly helper
Length60 minutes
Group4 to 10 learners, with one volunteer for every 2 or 3 people

Purpose

This session sits inside the program after the eLearning. It turns what learners saw in the course into practice they can use on the job.

Learning outcomes

  1. Use the mouse or touchpad, the main keys and windows with confidence
  2. Answer, make and end a video call, and use mute and camera buttons
  3. Spot the signs of a scam message or call, including AI voice scams
  4. Use the Stop, Check, Ask routine and know where to get help in Australia

Before the session

  • Ask each learner what they would most like to do, such as call a grandchild
  • Set up the practice video-call account in advance
  • Check the room is quiet, well lit and has good Wi-Fi
  • Brief volunteers: hands off the mouse, let the learner do it

Materials

  • Laptops or tablets, or learners’ own devices, with headphones
  • Printed summary cards in large print
  • A volunteer phone or tablet set up for practice video calls
  • Printed examples of real-looking (fictional) scam messages
  • Tea, coffee and name tags

Run sheet

TimeActivityFacilitator notes
0:00Welcome and cuppaCatch up. What did you try at home this week?
0:10One skill, shown slowlyDemonstrate one skill on a big screen, such as answering a call. Pause after every step.
0:20Your turnLearners practise in the course or on their own device. Volunteers help, but don’t take over.
0:40Safe or scam?Look at printed messages together. What gives each one away?
0:50Practice callEach learner makes a short video call to a volunteer.
0:57CloseEach person picks one thing to try at home this week.

Discussion prompts

  • What would you like to be able to do by the end?
  • How did it feel the first time you answered the call?
  • What made that message look suspicious?
  • Who could you call this week?

Facilitation tips

  • Go slowly, and repeat. Let learners use the mouse themselves.
  • Use plain words: “the button with the red phone”, not “end call icon”.
  • Never ask for, or look at, anyone’s passwords or bank details.
  • Some learners have been scammed before. Be kind, and point them to their bank, Scamwatch or IDCARE.
  • Celebrate every success. Confidence is the main outcome.

After the session

Send participants the follow-up tasks in the pre- and post-program activities, and share the manager embedding guide with their managers.
