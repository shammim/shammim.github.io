Part A · Multiple choice

Choose the best answer.

1. Your phone shows a video call from your daughter. Which button answers it?
  1. The red button
  2. The green button
  3. The microphone button
  4. The camera button
2. You want to stop the other person hearing you for a moment. What do you press?
  1. The red button
  2. The camera button
  3. The microphone button
  4. The volume button
3. A text says your parcel is waiting and asks you to click a link and pay $2. What should you do?
  1. Click the link and pay
  2. Reply to ask for more details
  3. Don’t click; check with the delivery company yourself
  4. Forward it to friends
4. Someone calls sounding like your grandson. He says he’s in trouble and needs money now. What do you do?
  1. Send the money quickly
  2. Hang up and call him back on the number you already have
  3. Give your bank details
  4. Keep talking until he explains
5. Which is the best password?
  1. Your name and birth year
  2. 123456
  3. Three random words with a number, like “kettle-garden-river-7”
  4. The word “password”

Marking guide (for assessors)

Part A answers

  1. B. The green button Green answers a call and red ends it.
  2. C. The microphone button The microphone button mutes you.
  3. C. Don’t click; check with the delivery company yourself Don’t click. Contact the company using details you already have.
  4. B. Hang up and call him back on the number you already have Scammers can copy voices with AI. Hang up and call back on a number you know.
  5. C. Three random words with a number, like “kettle-garden-river-7” Long, random words are strong and easier to remember.

Part B model answers

  1. Stop: don’t click, pay or share. Check: contact the person or company yourself. Ask: talk to someone you trust first.
  2. Call your bank straight away if money is involved, and report it to Scamwatch.

Part C: look for

  • Notices the urgency and the spelling mistake
  • Does not click the link or enter any details
  • Checks by calling the bank on the number on their card
  • Talks to someone they trust
  • Knows they can report it to Scamwatch

Result: all Part D criteria observed, and Parts A to C answered to the standard above. Where something is not yet shown, give feedback and agree a time to reassess.
