Facilitator guide

Asthma Management: workplace scenario program for hospital, aged care and NDIS staff
Hospital nurses, aged care nurses and care staff, and NDIS support workers

SessionBreathe easy: inhaler and asthma first aid skills
Length60 minutes
Group4 to 12 staff

Purpose

This session sits inside the program after the eLearning. It turns what learners saw in the course into practice they can use on the job.

Learning outcomes

  1. Notice early warning signs and triggers of worsening asthma
  2. Check and coach inhaler and spacer technique
  3. Follow the person’s asthma action plan, and asthma first aid in an emergency
  4. Escalate early using ISBAR

Before the session

  • Participants finish the micro-lesson for their setting first
  • Check placebo devices are clean and labelled
  • Confirm your organisation’s escalation and medication policies

Materials

  • Placebo puffers and spacers
  • Sample asthma action plans
  • Asthma first aid poster
  • ISBAR card
  • Learner workbook

Run sheet

TimeActivityFacilitator notes
0:00What did you notice?Replay the opening of the scenario. Ask: what were the early signs?
0:10Device practicePairs practise puffer and spacer technique with placebo devices, using teach-back.
0:25Reliever or preventer?Sort devices and explain each in plain language (Workbook activity 2).
0:35Asthma first aidWalk through the four steps: sit upright, give 4 separate puffs of reliever via spacer, wait 4 minutes, repeat; call 000 if no improvement.
0:45Escalate with ISBARPractise an ISBAR call about a falling peak flow.
0:55CloseEach person names one thing to check on their next shift.

Discussion prompts

  • What early signs did the person show before they said anything?
  • How would you check someone’s technique without making them feel tested?
  • When would you call 000?
  • What does the person’s own action plan say?

Facilitation tips

  • Content follows National Asthma Council Australia guidance. Always follow the person’s action plan and your organisation’s policy.
  • In NDIS settings, keep the participant’s choice and control at the centre.
  • Use placebo devices only.

After the session

Send participants the follow-up tasks in the pre- and post-program activities, and share the manager embedding guide with their managers.
