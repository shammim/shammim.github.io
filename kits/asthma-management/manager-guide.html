Manager embedding guide

Asthma Management: workplace scenario program for hospital, aged care and NDIS staff
Hospital nurses, aged care nurses and care staff, and NDIS support workers

Learning only changes practice when it is used, noticed and reinforced on the job. This guide gives you three short conversations and a simple plan.

What your team member will learn

  • Notice early warning signs and triggers of worsening asthma
  • Check and coach inhaler and spacer technique
  • Follow the person’s asthma action plan, and asthma first aid in an emergency
  • Escalate early using ISBAR

Before the program (10 minutes)

  • Who do you support who has asthma?
  • When did you last see someone use their inhaler?
  • What would make you hesitate to escalate?

After the program (15 minutes)

  • Talk me through what you would do at 3 am if someone was breathless.
  • Show me how you check technique.
  • What will you look out for on your next shift?

What good looks like on the job

  • Recognises early warning signs
  • Checks and coaches device technique using teach-back
  • Follows the action plan
  • Knows asthma first aid steps
  • Escalates using ISBAR
