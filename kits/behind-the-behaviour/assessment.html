1. Which is a factual observation?

2. What does the iceberg model show?

3. A toddler bites at a crowded pack-away every day. What is the best first step?

  2. Change the pack-away routine to reduce crowding, and observe
  3. Tell the family to talk to the child
  4. Ignore it
4. Which phrase is strengths-based?
  1. He’s aggressive
  2. She’s attention-seeking
  3. He’s finding it hard to manage big feelings when the room is busy
  4. She never listens

  1. Ask lots of questions to find out more
  2. Tell the family at pick-up
  3. Listen, write down their exact words and tell your nominated supervisor straight away
  4. Wait for more evidence

Marking guide (for assessors)

Part A answers

  1. B. At 11:40, Noah bit Ava’s arm during pack-away, then lay on the floor It records time, setting and exactly what happened.
  2. B. That the visible behaviour may come from needs we cannot see The behaviour sits above the surface; needs such as tiredness or overwhelm sit below.
  3. B. Change the pack-away routine to reduce crowding, and observe Change the antecedent first, then observe whether the behaviour changes.
  4. C. He’s finding it hard to manage big feelings when the room is busy It names the challenge without labelling the child.
  5. C. Listen, write down their exact words and tell your nominated supervisor straight away Follow your service’s child safe policy and mandatory reporting obligations.

Part B model answers

  1. Behaviour often responds to triggers; changing the antecedent (crowding, waiting, noise) can remove the need for the behaviour and avoids blaming the child.
  2. Choose a private moment and time; start with something positive and true; share facts not labels; ask for their view; agree a small next step.

Part C: look for

  • Shares facts, not labels
  • Asks about what the family notices at home
  • Suggests one small change to try (e.g. her own cushion spot)
  • Agrees when to check in again

Result: all Part D criteria observed, and Parts A to C answered to the standard above. Where something is not yet shown, give feedback and agree a time to reassess.
