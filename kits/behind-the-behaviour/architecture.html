Training architecture

Behind the Behaviour: behaviour guidance and family partnership program
Cert III and Diploma educators and graduate early childhood teachers

AudienceCert III and Diploma educators and graduate early childhood teachers
Program length6 weeks · 2.5-hour eLearning in modules · two 90-minute workshops · team practice
Evidence of learningAssessment, observation checklist and manager conversation

Program outcomes

1Read behaviour as communication and identify the possible need behind it
2Record factual ABC observations without labels
3Change the environment or routine before responding to the behaviour
4Raise a concern with a family without blame, and know when to escalate
