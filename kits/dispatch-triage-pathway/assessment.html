Marking guide (for assessors)

Part A answers

  1. B. So everyone completes the baseline before learning, for a fair pre/post comparison The baseline must come first for Level 2 evaluation to mean anything.
  2. C. Level 3: behaviour on the job It verifies behaviour on the floor.
  3. B. A calm voice, their name if known, and one clear question at a time Calm, clear and one task at a time.
  4. B. Correct the code straight away following protocol and tell your supervisor Correct promptly and escalate as your protocol requires.
  5. B. Meeting the completion conditions set on the course page Moodle completion conditions control the certificate.

Part B model answers

  1. So changes in score reflect learning, not a different difficulty.
  2. Calm, slow voice; acknowledge emotion; use their name; one clear question or task at a time.

Part C: look for

  • Uses de-escalation to regain control of the call
  • Gathers the information needed to code accurately
  • Applies the correct priority per protocol
  • Documents and escalates as required

Result: all Part D criteria observed, and Parts A to C answered to the standard above. Where something is not yet shown, give feedback and agree a time to reassess.
