Part A · Multiple choice

Choose the best answer.

1. You are asked to step into the kitchen during pack-away. What do you do first?
  1. Go quickly and come straight back
  2. Hand over your zone and wait for a colleague to confirm
  3. Ask the children to sit until you return
  4. Close the door so children cannot follow
2. What does active supervision mean?
  1. Staying in one spot with a clear view
  2. Knowing where every child is and what they are doing, ready to respond
  3. Counting children every 30 minutes
  4. Watching only the children most likely to get hurt
3. When is supervision risk usually highest in a toddler room?
  1. Quiet play
  2. Story time
  3. Transitions between activities or spaces
  4. Rest time
4. Which National Quality Standard element covers supervision?
  1. Element 2.2.1
  2. Element 5.2.2
  3. Element 6.1.1
  4. Element 7.1.1
5. A child is distressed at the door during a transition. What is the best response?
  1. Leave the room to settle the child
  2. Comfort the child while keeping both zones in sight, and call for support
  3. Wait until the transition is over
  4. Ask an older child to watch the others

6. Explain two ways you position yourself to keep children in sight.
7. Describe what you say and do when handing over supervision.

Marking guide (for assessors)

Part A answers

  1. B. Hand over your zone and wait for a colleague to confirm Supervision passes to another educator only when they confirm they have your zone.
  2. B. Knowing where every child is and what they are doing, ready to respond Active supervision is constant awareness and readiness to act, not a fixed position.
  3. C. Transitions between activities or spaces Transitions split children and adults across spaces, so lines of sight break.
  4. A. Element 2.2.1 Element 2.2.1: adequate supervision ensures children are protected from harm and hazard.
  5. B. Comfort the child while keeping both zones in sight, and call for support Respond to the child without leaving other children unsupervised.

Part B model answers

  1. Back to the wall facing into the room; standing at the threshold between zones; scanning regularly; avoiding long tasks with your back turned.
  2. Name the zone and children, wait for the colleague to confirm, do not leave until they accept, say when you will be back.

Part C: look for

  • Keeps line of sight and ratio for both zones
  • Asks the parent to wait or calls for support rather than leaving
  • Recounts children after the transition
  • Communicates clearly with the co-educator

Result: all Part D criteria observed, and Parts A to C answered to the standard above. Where something is not yet shown, give feedback and agree a time to reassess.
