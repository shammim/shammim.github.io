Facilitator guide

Microsoft Excel: Intermediate to Advanced skills program
Staff who already use Excel every week

SessionClinic 1: Reliable data
Length60 minutes
Group6 to 12 staff with laptops

Purpose

This session sits inside the program after the eLearning. It turns what learners saw in the course into practice they can use on the job.

Learning outcomes

  1. Structure data as Excel Tables with validation so it stays reliable
  2. Look up and summarise data with XLOOKUP, dynamic arrays and PivotTables
  3. Answer what-if questions with Goal Seek and scenarios
  4. Protect and share workbooks safely, and automate a repetitive task

Before the session

  • Participants complete Module 1 first
  • Ask them to bring a de-identified copy of a real workbook
  • Check everyone can open the practice file

Materials

  • Laptops with Excel installed
  • Practice sales workbook
  • Formula Quick Reference
  • Learner workbook

Run sheet

TimeActivityFacilitator notes
0:00Why totals breakShow a range where new rows are missed by a SUM. Ask: who has seen this?
0:10Make it a TableEveryone converts the practice data to a Table and names it.
0:20Structured referencesRewrite a formula using column names. Add a row and watch it update.
0:30ValidationAdd drop-down lists and date rules so bad data can’t get in.
0:45Your own workbookApply the same steps to the workbook you brought.
0:55CloseEach person notes the next fix for their project.

Discussion prompts

  • Where does your data come from, and who adds to it?
  • What breaks most often in your workbooks?
  • Which column would benefit from a drop-down?
  • Who else uses this file?

Facilitation tips

  • Use de-identified data only.
  • Let people work at their own pace; pair confident users with newer ones.
  • Save a copy before making structural changes.

After the session

Send participants the follow-up tasks in the pre- and post-program activities, and share the manager embedding guide with their managers.
