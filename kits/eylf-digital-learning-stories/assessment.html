Part A · Multiple choice

Choose the best answer.

1. Which sentence is an objective observation?
  1. Leo was really clever today
  2. Leo built a tower of eight blocks and rebuilt it twice when it fell
  3. Leo is a natural engineer
  4. Leo was much better than usual
2. A child whose family has not consented is in the background of a photo. What should you do?
  1. Publish it, the child is in the background
  2. Blur or crop the child, or choose another image
  3. Ask the child if it is OK
  4. Publish it to staff only
3. Which EYLF V2.0 outcome relates to persistence and problem solving?
  1. Outcome 1: identity
  2. Outcome 2: community
  3. Outcome 4: confident and involved learners
  4. Outcome 5: communication
4. A story is published to every family by mistake. What do you do first?
  1. Wait to see if anyone notices
  2. Retract it and tell your nominated supervisor straight away
  3. Email families asking them to delete it
  4. Change the child’s name
5. Which law sets the Australian Privacy Principles?
  1. Privacy Act 1988
  2. Fair Work Act 2009
  3. Education and Care Services National Law
  4. Child Wellbeing and Safety Act

Marking guide (for assessors)

Part A answers

  1. B. Leo built a tower of eight blocks and rebuilt it twice when it fell It describes what the child did, without judgement.
  2. B. Blur or crop the child, or choose another image Images of children without consent must not be shared.
  3. C. Outcome 4: confident and involved learners Outcome 4 covers dispositions such as persistence, curiosity and problem solving.
  4. B. Retract it and tell your nominated supervisor straight away Contain the breach first, then follow your service’s breach procedure.
  5. A. Privacy Act 1988 The Privacy Act 1988 sets out the APPs, including use and disclosure (APP 6) and security (APP 11).

Part B model answers

  1. Describes what the child can do; uses the child’s words and actions; avoids comparison and labels; links to learning and next steps.
  2. Consent for every identifiable child; no other children identifiable; no sensitive personal details; correct audience; accurate outcome links.

Part C: look for

  • Identifies the judging language
  • Rewrites using what Mia did
  • Chooses an accurate outcome link with reasons
  • Removes or redacts the child without consent

Result: all Part D criteria observed, and Parts A to C answered to the standard above. Where something is not yet shown, give feedback and agree a time to reassess.
