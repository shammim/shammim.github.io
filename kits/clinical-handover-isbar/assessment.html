Marking guide (for assessors)

Part A answers

  1. B. Introduction Introduce yourself, your role and location, and identify the patient.
  2. B. Her NEWS2 is 10. I need you to review her within 15 minutes. Should I take blood cultures and a lactate? It is specific, time-bound and invites agreement on actions.
  3. C. Three Three approved identifiers, such as full name, date of birth and URN.
  4. C. Standard 6 Standard 6: Communicating for Safety.
  5. B. To confirm both people understand the plan Read-back confirms the plan was heard and understood.

Part B model answers

  1. Assessment: what you think is happening, based on findings. Recommendation: what you need, from whom, by when.
  2. For example: “His blood pressure is 88/50, down from 120/80 two hours ago.”

Part C: look for

  • Introduces self and identifies the patient with three identifiers
  • States the situation and observations as numbers
  • Gives a reasoned assessment
  • Makes a time-bound recommendation and requests read-back

Result: all Part D criteria observed, and Parts A to C answered to the standard above. Where something is not yet shown, give feedback and agree a time to reassess.
