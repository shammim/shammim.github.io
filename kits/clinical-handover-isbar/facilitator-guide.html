Facilitator guide

Clinical Handover with ISBAR: communicating for safety program
Registered and enrolled nurses

SessionSay it so it sticks: ISBAR simulation
Length60 minutes
Group4 to 12 nurses

Purpose

This session sits inside the program after the eLearning. It turns what learners saw in the course into practice they can use on the job.

Learning outcomes

  1. Give a structured handover using all five ISBAR components
  2. Identify the patient using three approved identifiers
  3. Use numbers and early warning scores, not adjectives
  4. Make a clear, time-bound recommendation and confirm with read-back

Before the session

  • Participants complete the eLearning first
  • Use your health service’s own early warning tool on the scenario charts
  • Brief a colleague to play the registrar

Materials

  • Patient scenario cards with observation charts
  • ISBAR Handover job aid
  • Phones or a mock call setup
  • Learner workbook

Run sheet

0:50DebriefWhat made the recommendation clear? What was vague?
0:55CloseEach person picks one ISBAR habit for their next shift.

Discussion prompts

  • Which three identifiers did you use?
  • Where did you use a number instead of an adjective?
  • What exactly did you ask for, and by when?
  • How did you confirm the plan?

Facilitation tips

  • Use your health service’s escalation tool and policy.
  • Keep scenarios fictional or fully de-identified.
  • Praise specific, measurable language.

After the session

Send participants the follow-up tasks in the pre- and post-program activities, and share the manager embedding guide with their managers.
