Program kit · Microsoft Word

Facilitator guide

Microsoft Word: Build Better Documents program
Office staff who write or edit long documents

SessionDocument clinic: fix it once, fix it for everyone
Length60 minutes
Group6 to 12 staff with laptops

Purpose

This session sits inside the program after the eLearning. It turns what learners saw in the course into practice they can use on the job.

Learning outcomes

  1. Use the approved template and heading styles consistently
  2. Generate an accurate, automatic contents page
  3. Control layout with section breaks and page numbering
  4. Share drafts with version history and export an accessible, tagged PDF

Before the session

  • Participants complete Lessons 1 to 3 first
  • Ask for one de-identified team document to fix
  • Confirm where the approved template lives

Materials

  • Laptops with Word
  • The messy practice report
  • Approved template
  • Word job aids
  • Learner workbook

Run sheet

TimeActivityFacilitator notes
0:00Why documents breakOpen the practice report with formatting marks on. Ask: what is causing the blank page?
0:10Styles, not formattingApply Heading 1 and 2 to the report. Open the Navigation Pane.
0:20Automatic contentsInsert and update a contents page.
0:30Sections and page numbersAdd a section break and restart numbering correctly.
0:40Share and exportUse version history, then export a tagged PDF with bookmarks.
0:55CloseEach person names the document they will fix next.

Discussion prompts

  • What does show/hide reveal about this page?
  • Why does a bold, bigger line not appear in the contents?
  • Who edits this document after you?
  • How will a screen reader user navigate the PDF?

Facilitation tips

  • Turn on formatting marks early; they explain most problems.
  • Use the organisation’s approved template.
  • Check PDF accessibility with Word’s Accessibility Checker.

After the session

Send participants the follow-up tasks in the pre- and post-program activities, and share the manager embedding guide with their managers.
