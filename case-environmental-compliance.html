Environmental Compliance & Land Management

Gets new field trainees safe and ready before their first placement, and set up a reusable course template that every onboarding module in the series now shares.

View storyboard Job aids Course
Audience
Entry-level conservation and land management trainees, apprentices and new field staff
Format

Outcomes

Accessibility

My role

What I did on this project

Workplace project, built for an organisation’s internal staff.

RoleLearning designer & developer
Project typeWorkplace project
What I didNeeds and learner analysis, six outcomes, image hotspot, flip cards, Jordan role-play and feedback, knowledge check, reusable course template with 18 content blocks, voice-over scripts, SCORM player, case study carousel
ToolsHTML, CSS and JavaScript · SCORM 1.2 and 2004 · AI voice-over
TeamSolo, end to end. Course built for the organisation’s internal staff.

The challenge

Trainees need to understand safety protocols, biosecurity and workplace expectations before they set foot on site. The organisation also needed one consistent, accessible experience for every onboarding module in the series.

The approach

An end-to-end process: need, learner analysis, objectives, design, development and evaluation. Bite-sized chunks paired with authentic field scenarios.

Learn by doing

Each responsibility is a step learners move through. The pull-out menu tracks progress and locks lessons until the activities are done.

Interactive features

Image hotspot, flip cards, an AI workplace scenario with a team leader, contextual feedback against every aim, and a five-question knowledge check.
