Working together

How a project runs

Clear steps, fixed quotes and two review rounds.

  1. 1

    Discovery call

    A free 30-minute conversation about the problem, your learners, timing and budget.

  2. 2

    Proposal and fixed quote

    Scope, deliverables, milestones and price in writing, so there are no surprises.

  3. 3

    Design sign-off

    Training architecture, objectives and a storyboard or prototype for you to approve before the build.

  4. 4

    Build and review

    I build everything, with two review rounds with your subject matter expert.

  5. 5

    Hand-over

    SCORM package and source files, facilitator and manager resources, and a walkthrough video of the finished course.

What I need from you

  • Time from a subject matter expert for two reviews
  • Your existing content, policies and procedures
  • Your LMS details, or advice on which format suits you
  • One decision-maker for sign-off
