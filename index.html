What Makes Me Different

I design for real-world application

My apporach goes beyond delivering information. I focus on creating learning experiences that help learners understand , practice and appply new skills in authentic workplace situations.

I bridge technology and learning

With a background in Computer Science, teaching, and technical training, I understand both the technology behind digital tools and the learning principles needed to make them accessible and meaningful.

I think like a learner

I start by asking: Who are the learners? What do they already know? What do they need to do differently? This learner-first mindset helps me create experiences that are relevant, practical, and purposeful.

I combine structure with creativity

I bring together instructional design, visual communication, UX thinking, and digital tools to create learning experiences that are not only effective but also intuitive and engaging.

I design, test, reflect and improve

I see instructional design as an iterative process. I use feedback, assessment evidence, and reflection to continuously improve the learning experience.

I bring a teacher's perspective

My teaching background has taught me that learners don't all learn in the same way. I design with clarity, scaffolding, active participation, and accessibility in mind.
