My Approach

What Makes Me Different

Design for real-world application

I go beyond delivering information. I create experiences that empower learners to understand, practice, and apply new skills in authentic workplace situations.

Bridge technology & learning

With a background in Computer Science and teaching, I understand both the technology behind digital platforms and the pedagogical principles that make them effective.

Think like a learner

I always ask: Who are the learners? What do they already know? What behavior needs to change? This ensures every module is relevant, clear, and purposeful.

Structure with creativity

I combine instructional design frameworks, UX thinking, visual communication, and digital authoring tools to build clean, intuitive, and interactive modules.

Design, test, reflect & iterate

Instructional design is iterative. I utilize qualitative feedback, assessment evidence, and analytics to continuously refine and optimize learning outcomes.

Educator's perspective

Classroom and workshop experience taught me that learners need varied pacing, scaffolding, and active engagement to succeed.

Project Overview

HTML Essentials – Interactive Microlearning Module | Instructional & UX Designer

Roles: Instructional & UX Designer

Challenge & Context: Addressed cognitive overload experienced by beginner technical learners when navigating web development syntax and semantic document structure.

Instructional Strategy: Applied ADDIE and cognitive chunking principles to structure core HTML5 concepts into modular units (5–7 mins) with real-time interactive code sandboxes.

UX & Accessibility: Designed user journeys, visual wireframes, and prototypes aligned with Google UX principles and WCAG accessibility standards for seamless engagement.

Assessment & Impact: Integrated formative scenario-based knowledge checks and instant-feedback coding exercises, driving measurable knowledge retention and practical mastery.

Project Overview

Identifying IP, Ethics & Privacy in ICT Environments | Instructional Designer & Curriculum Developer

Roles: Instructional Designer & Curriculum Developer

Challenge & Context: Designed a compliance-oriented instructional unit to help IT professionals navigate complex legal, intellectual property, copyright, and privacy obligations.

Instructional Strategy: Developed authentic scenario-based branching case studies reflecting Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), digital copyright, and ethical data handling dilemmas.

Resource Architecture: Authored comprehensive facilitator guides, learner workbooks, interactive decision trees, and digital job aids for workplace application.

Assessment & Evaluation: Designed rubric-aligned workplace simulations and formative situational judgment tests (SJTs), ensuring verifiable compliance and critical thinking.

Project Overview

Securely Manage PII & Workplace Information | Instructional Designer & Security Specialist

Roles: Instructional Designer & Security Specialist

Challenge & Context: Mitigated enterprise data breaches and improper handling of Personally Identifiable Information (PII) across hybrid workplace operations.

Instructional Strategy: Designed an interactive cybersecurity & information security module utilizing authentic breach scenarios, data classification exercises, and lifecycle management workflows.

Resource Architecture: Developed clear information-handling matrices, standard operating procedure (SOP) quick-reference guides, and simulated phishing/data breach decision modules.

Assessment & Impact: Implemented performance-based scenario assessments evaluating risk identification, secure storage protocols, and incident escalation procedures.
