Report Season: Noticing Strain Early

A branching workplace scenario about psychosocial safety. Tom notices that his colleague Priya is not herself in report season, and the learner decides what he does next. Every choice plays out.

Challenge & context

Sustained high workload is a psychosocial hazard. In report season, colleagues notice the strain but often joke it off or quietly take on the work, which hides the problem from the people who can change it.

Instructional strategy

One short scene instead of a slide on definitions. The learner is Tom, a Year 4 teacher, in the staffroom at 5:40 pm in week 8 of term 2. They watch the conversation with Priya, choose what Tom does, see the consequence and can come back to try every option.

Content & compliance

Frames workload as a hazard to manage, not a personal failing: notice the early signs, check in properly and raise the workload with the team leader. The characters are fictional, and the course is not affiliated with the Victorian Department of Education.

Interactive features

An illustrated chat played line by line with Pause, Next line and Restart, optional character voices, a three-way decision, consequences for each choice, a debrief that explains every option and a private reflection prompt.

RS-01Scene card

Report season

On screen
Primary school staffroom, 5:40 pm. “You’re Tom. Watch the conversation in the staffroom, then decide what Tom does next.” Priya Sharma and Tom Nguyen, Year 4 teachers.
Interaction
Start the scene. Voices can be switched on or off, and there is a Theme toggle.
Audio
Optional character voices from the browser.
RS-02Illustrated chat

The staffroom conversation

On screen
Priya: “Did I? I’ve been sleeping about five hours. I snapped at a student today over nothing.” A new email from a parent arrives.
Interaction
Lines appear one at a time with stage directions. Pause, Next line and Restart; a progress bar shows the step out of 16.
Audio
Optional voices, read line by line.
RS-03Decision

What should Tom do?

On screen
A: “Hang in there, we all suffer through report season.” B: Tom sits down, checks in properly and encourages Priya to raise the workload with their team leader. C: Tom offers to write her remaining comments himself and keep it between them.
Interaction
Choose one option. Each plays out in a consequence scene the following Thursday, and the learner can come back to try the others.
Audio
None.
RS-04Debrief

Debrief and reflection

On screen
Why B is stronger: A normalises the strain, C hides the problem and moves the hazard onto Tom, B raises it with someone who can change the work.
Interaction
A private reflection: “When have you told someone, or yourself, ‘it’s just that time of year’? What could you say instead?” Only the learner sees it.
Audio
None.
