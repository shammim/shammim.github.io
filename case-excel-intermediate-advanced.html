CS-06 · Microsoft technology series

Microsoft Excel: Intermediate to Advanced

A two-hour, 13-lesson course that follows on from the Excel Refresher. Learners practise in a live grid with a formula bar, and each lesson unlocks the next.

View storyboard Job aids Course
Audience
Staff who already use Excel every week
Format
Custom HTML course with a live practice grid
Structure
4 modules, 13 lessons and a final assessment
Assessment
Knowledge checks with hints; final assessment, 80% to pass
Series
Follows the Excel Refresher in the Microsoft technology series

Challenge & context

Learners finished the Excel Refresher, but their lists have grown to thousands of rows and totals keep breaking when someone adds data below the formulas.

Instructional strategy

Every lesson opens with a workplace problem, shows the fix in a live grid, steps through it in tabs and ends with a knowledge check. Lessons unlock in order.

Content & governance

Builds reliable data habits first (Tables, validation, Power Query) so formulas, PivotTables and dashboards stay accurate and safe to share.

Interactive features

A spreadsheet-style grid with formula bar, step tabs, hints that open on request, and a final assessment with an 80% pass mark.
