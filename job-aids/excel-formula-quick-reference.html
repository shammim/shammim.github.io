1 · Reliable data

Turn a range into a TableCtrl + T, then name it on Table Design
Total a column by name=SUM(tblSales[Revenue])
Restrict entries to a listData › Data Validation › List
Clean an export you get every monthData › Get Data › Power Query. Record the steps once, then Refresh

2 · Lookups

Find a value, with a not-found message=XLOOKUP(A2,tblStaff[ID],tblStaff[Dept],"Not found")
Older files=INDEX(C:C,MATCH(A2,A:A,0))
Catch any error=IFERROR(formula,"Check ID")

3 · Dynamic arrays and LET

Rows that meet a condition=FILTER(tblSales,tblSales[Region]="North")
A distinct list, sorted=SORT(UNIQUE(tblSales[Rep]))
Name a step inside a formula=LET(r,tblSales[Revenue],SUM(r)/COUNT(r))
