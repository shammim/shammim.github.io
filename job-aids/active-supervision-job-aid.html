Job aid · Toddler room transitions

Active Supervision
Transition Job Aid

NQS 2.2.1 · National Law s.165 · Reg 123
Child Safe Principles 5 & 8

1 · Scan

Make eye contact with your co-educator. Count heads in every zone.

2 · Pivot

Say the ratio shift out loud: who is watching which zone.

3 · Handover

Move to the threshold so you can see both zones. Only move after the other person confirms.

Ratio quick reference (centre-based)

Birth to 24 months1:4
Over 24 months to under 36 months1:5 (Vic 1:4)
36 months to preschool age1:11 (state variations)

Ratios are counted for each room. When one educator leaves 8 toddlers, it’s 1:8.

Where to stand during a transition

  • Stand at the doorway threshold, facing both zones
  • Keep your back to a wall, never to the room
  • Close or check gates and latches before children move
  • Make sure someone has line of sight to climbing furniture and blind corners

Handover script

“Sam, ratio shift: I’ve got the doorway and the prep area, all 8 in sight. Can you take Oliver?” Wait for a nod or a “Got it” before you move.

Never

  • Leave the room “just for 30 seconds” when it puts the room out of ratio
  • Call a handover across a noisy room
  • Treat a gate or latch as supervision
  • Turn your back on the room to do paperwork during a transition

Instead

  • Ask the kitchen or a floater to bring trays to the door
  • Use the room phone or intercom for help
  • Pause the routine. Being late is fine; being out of ratio is not
  • If a child leaves unsupervised: locate the child, notify the nominated supervisor, and report to the regulatory authority within 24 hours
Phased induction: Days 1–5 shadowing (supernumerary) · Days 6–13 supported practice · Day 14 counted in ratio · Weeks 3–12 consolidation. Check ratios against your state’s current requirements.
