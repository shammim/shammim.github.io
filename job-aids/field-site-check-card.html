Field Site Check Card

Environmental Compliance & Land Management
Keep in your field kit

1 · Stop

Don't pull, cut or move a plant or material you can't identify.

2 · Explain

Say why it matters here: near a waterway, a protected area or native vegetation.

3 · Ask

Get guidance or expert identification from your team leader or ecologist.

4 · Record

Photo, location pin and a log entry in the reporting system.

Scan the site for

  • Damaged or trampled native vegetation
  • Rubbish and dumped waste
  • Vehicle tracks and parking near the waterway
  • Fuel, oil or chemical spills
  • Plants that look out of place or are spreading fast

Before you leave

  • Use the wash-down point for boots, tools and vehicles
  • Take all waste with you
  • Check your log entries are complete
  • Tell your team leader about anything unresolved

Reporting script

“Jordan, I've found a plant I can't identify near the creek. I haven't touched it. I've taken a photo, dropped a pin and logged it. Can we get it identified before anyone removes it?”

Never

  • Remove a plant before it's identified. Some native species are protected.
  • Leave a site without washing down when you've been near weeds
  • Leave rubbish or spills unreported
Check this card against your organisation's biosecurity, waste and reporting procedures and any site-specific permit conditions.
