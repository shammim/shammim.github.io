Instead of a label, write the facts

Not: “Noah was naughty at pack-away.”
Write: At 11:40 during pack-away, Noah bit Ava’s arm, then screamed and lay on the floor.

Not: “Mia is attention-seeking.”
Write: Mia called “look at me” six times in ten minutes while I set up lunch.

Look for patterns across several sheets

  • Same time of day? Before meals, late afternoon, after a long day?
  • Same place or transition? Crowded, noisy, rushed?
  • Same people or activity?
  • Is it new, more frequent or more intense? Has a skill been lost?
