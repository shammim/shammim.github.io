Frontline Workplace Conflict De-Escalation & Resolution Simulation

Sector & audienceFrontline services · council, retail and health reception staff

Target behaviour changeStay calm, acknowledge and set limits with upset customers, and move away to get help when behaviour turns threatening.

Ecosystem deliverables
  • 32-minute course with three branching scenarios
  • CALM Pocket Guide
  • Kirkpatrick evaluation plan and day-30 observation checklist
  • Articulate 360 and Vyond production plan

My roleLearning experience designer and developer: needs and behaviour targets, the CALM model, branching scripts, plain-English rewrites of SME procedures, scenes, voice-over, final check, evaluation plan and SCORM packaging.

Start the courseAll case studies

Engaging learning experience

  • A tension meter that reacts to every choice
  • Rewind to any decision
  • A results card across all three scenarios

Technical stack

  • Custom HTML5, CSS and JavaScript course build
  • SCORM 1.2 and 2004
  • Production plan for Articulate 360
  • Illustrated scenes
  • AI voice-over
  • Vyond production plan and storyboard
  • CALM Pocket Guide

Accessibility

  • WCAG 2.1 AA and UDL: keys 1–3 to choose, no timers
  • Voice-over with transcript; tension shown in words and icons as well as colour
  • Automated WCAG 2.2 AA check (axe-core) on the opening screen: no issues found

At a glance

Audience
Frontline staff in local government, retail, and health and community services (Part 1); L&D, WHS and operations leads (Part 2)
Format
Rise-style course with three branching scenarios, illustrated scenes and AI voice-over · SCORM 1.2 and 2004
Structure
10 lessons and a final check, about 32 minutes · 3 scenarios, 5 decisions each, 10 endings
Assessment
Confidence check before and after, scenario endings, 8-question final check (80% to pass), day-30 observation checklist
Accessibility
WCAG 2.1 AA and UDL: voice-over with transcript, keys 1–3 to choose, no timers, tension shown in words and icons as well as colour

Highlights

  • Animation

    The tension meter reacts as voices rise, and speaking indicators show who’s talking in each illustrated scene.

  • Design system

    Built with my SS Learning Design system (AI-assisted, created with Claude): Inter and Lato type, colour tokens and the Rise-style course template.
