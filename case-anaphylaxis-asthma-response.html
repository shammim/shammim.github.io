Acute Anaphylaxis & Asthma Response

Sector & audienceEarly childhood education and care · lead educators and first aid officers

Target behaviour changeGive adrenaline without hesitating when a child shows signs of anaphylaxis, following the ASCIA Action Plan step by step.

Ecosystem deliverables
  • Four-lesson course with a 60-second response simulation
  • Quick Response Card for the medical cabinet
  • End-of-course compliance report for the service leader

My roleLearning experience designer and developer: mapped the ASCIA response to decisions, designed the timed simulation and its variables, built the course and card, and tested accessibility.

Start the courseAll case studies

Engaging learning experience

  • A 60-second countdown with a stability score that falls while you hesitate
  • A medical cabinet that unlocks at the right moment
  • Feedback at the moment of error, with every step logged as xAPI

Technical stack

  • Custom HTML5, CSS and JavaScript course build
  • xAPI statements for every step
  • Printable Quick Response Card

Accessibility

  • Built to WCAG 2.1 AA
  • Accessibility and functional testing as part of the build

At a glance

Audience

Highlights

  • Animation

    The countdown pulses as time runs low and the stability bar drains, so pressure is felt, not described.

  • Design system

    Built with my SS Learning Design system (AI-assisted, created with Claude): Inter and Lato type, colour tokens and the same course template as Active Supervision.
