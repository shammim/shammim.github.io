Acute Anaphylaxis & Asthma Response

A timed, high-stakes simulation where educators have 60 seconds to recognise anaphylaxis at the lunch table, unlock the medical cabinet and start the ASCIA response.

Challenge & context

Anaphylaxis can become life-threatening within minutes. At the lunch table there is no time to look anything up, so educators need to recognise the signs and follow the ASCIA Action Plan straight away.

Instructional strategy

Context, then sign recognition, then a timed Test Me. The learner plays the lead educator and first aid officer in a preschool room with 22 children and three ASCIA plans.

Content & compliance

Mapped to NQF Regulation 136 (first aid qualifications), ASCIA Action Plans for Anaphylaxis, the anaphylaxis-plus-asthma rule, Regulation 94 and incident records under Regulation 87.

Interactive features

Custom variables, timed decision layers and a falling patient stability score. A wrong call opens a feedback layer, and every step is logged as an xAPI statement.
