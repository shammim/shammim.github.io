VET Onboarding: Your First 30 Days

Shows new VET students what their first 30 days look like, from enrolment to their first assessment, who to go to when something goes wrong, and what is expected of them.

At a glance

Context
Vocational education and training
Audience
People starting a Certificate or Diploma with an RTO or TAFE, including first-time and returning learners
Format
Self-paced onboarding module on the series course template, SCORM 1.2 and 2004
Structure
3 modules, a practical scenario and a five-question final quiz, about 25 minutes
Resources
Day 1 to Day 30 timeline, 30-day checklist, glossary of VET terms, study habits and wellbeing
Accessibility
Voice-over, captions and transcript on every section; keyboard ordering with no dragging; shortcuts

The challenge

New VET students often don’t know what happens when, or who to ask. Small problems in the first month, like a login that fails, an assessment they don’t understand or a missed class, can quietly turn into a reason to leave.

The approach

The course is built around the first 30 days: a Day 1 to Day 30 timeline, the eight-stage journey from Enrol to Progress, and the four people and teams who can help. Each module ends with the learner applying it.

Content

Who can support you, the onboarding journey, learner responsibilities, study habits and wellbeing (with Lifeline and 000), and a glossary of VET terms such as RTO, USI, RPL and Not Yet Competent. It points learners back to their own provider’s policies.

Interactive features

A clickable 30-day timeline, support role cards, a put-the-journey-in-order activity using move buttons, two knowledge checks, Sam’s first-fortnight scenario with three moments, a final quiz (80% to pass), a checklist that saves ticks and a “first step” reflection.
