Care That Counts: 30-Day Onboarding

A 30-day onboarding program for new aged and dementia care workers: 20 short screens, one thing to try on every shift, and check-ins at day 7, 15 and 30 that look at what workers can do, not what they clicked.

View storyboard Job aids Course
Audience
New care workers in residential, memory support, home care and respite
Format
Blended onboarding: custom HTML course on the series template, plus on-shift practice with a buddy
Structure
20 screens in four stages, about 3 hours online across 30 days
Standards
Aged Care Act 2024 · Statement of Rights · Code of Conduct for Aged Care · SIRS

Challenge & context

New care workers are often on the floor within days. Many bring real caring experience but no sector training, and compliance topics such as the Code of Conduct and serious incident reporting usually arrive as slides that change nothing on shift.

Instructional strategy

Twenty short screens of five to fifteen minutes, released over 30 days in four stages. Every screen ends with one thing to try on the next shift with a buddy, and three check-ins turn the online work into observed practice.

Content & compliance

Built around the Aged Care Act 2024 and its Statement of Rights, the Code of Conduct for Aged Care, the Serious Incident Response Scheme, ISBAR escalation, and the Quality Standard on food, nutrition and safe swallowing.

Interactive features

Tabs, flip cards, accordions, sorting, two image hotspots, step-by-step processes, branching conversations with slots for AI role-play, storyboarded video scenes, buddy and supervisor rubrics, a six-question quiz and a 30-day record. An Author view labels the template block behind every screen.

Screen-by-screen storyboard

Frames from the built course. Select a screen to see it full size.

CO-01Hero + journey

Welcome to care that counts

On screen
30 days · 20 screens · 3 hours online. A four-step journey: Welcome, Safe on every shift, Conversations that matter, Confident in complex care.
Interaction
Each stage opens when the last is done. Menu on the left, progress saved on the device, Previous and Next.
Audio
None on this screen.
CO-02Checklist + reflection

What you already bring

On screen
Eight statements, from “I have cared for a family member” to “I speak more than one language”.
Interaction
Tick at least three, then write at least ten words about a time you helped someone feel safe. Both are required.
Audio
None.
CO-04Accordion + sorting

Rights, dignity and the Code of Conduct

On screen
The eight Code of Conduct behaviours in plain words, then everyday moments such as “Talks about a resident’s toileting in the corridor”.
Interaction
Open all eight panels, then sort each moment into Respects dignity or Puts dignity at risk and check the answers.
Audio
None.
CO-05Image hotspot

Spot the risks in a resident’s room

On screen
An illustrated room with six markers: bed height and brakes, floor, call bell, skin and heels, hands and PPE.
Interaction
Select each marker to see what to check. A two-minute room checklist follows, then a next-shift takeaway.
Audio
None.
CO-07Process + job aid

Handover and escalation with ISBAR

On screen
Five steps with a care-worker example for each: “This is Ana, care worker on the memory unit, about Mr Patel in room 12.”
Interaction
Step through I-S-B-A-R, a Do and Avoid list for notes, the ISBAR pocket card with Copy as text, and a knowledge check with two attempts.
Audio
None.
CO-08Rubric

Day 7 check-in

On screen
Five areas, from Safe on shift to Looking after yourself, each rated Needs support, On track or Confident.
Interaction
The worker rates themselves, then agrees next week’s focus with their buddy and books when the buddy will watch.
Audio
None.
CO-10Video + branching

“I want to go home”

On screen
It is 4 pm and Mr Patel believes he needs to go home to his wife. Looking for: validate, do not argue, redirect gently.
Interaction
A storyboarded video scene, then a branching decision with three replies and feedback. The block is a slot for a live AI role-play.
Audio
Scene voice read aloud by the device, with captions, speed control and a transcript.
CO-16Image hotspot

Mealtimes and swallowing safely

On screen
A meal tray with the diet and fluid plan (soft, level 5) and five markers to check before helping someone eat.
Interaction
Explore all five markers, read the signs of choking, then keep the Mealtime check job aid.
Audio
None.
CO-20Quiz + record

Your 30-day record and next step

On screen
Six questions, from an unexplained bruise to when restraint can be used, then a record of screens, activities and minutes completed.
Interaction
Submit the quiz, then take the record to the next supervisor conversation and pick one skill to build next.
Audio
None.
