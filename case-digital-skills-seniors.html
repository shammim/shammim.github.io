Digital Skills for Seniors

Sector & audienceCommunity · older Australians new to computers or not yet confident online

Target behaviour changeMake a video call to family and spot a scam with confidence, at home or with a community program.

Ecosystem deliverables
  • 45-minute course that also runs offline from one file
  • Personal summary card
  • Links to Scamwatch and Be Connected

My roleLearning experience designer and developer: learner analysis for older adults, fading-support sequence, safe practice screens, confidence check-ins, narration scripts, summary card and build.

Start the courseAll case studies

Engaging learning experience

  • Support fades as confidence grows: guided lessons, then free choice
  • Safe practice: nothing is ever really sent
  • Confidence check-ins instead of a test

Technical stack

  • Custom HTML5, CSS and JavaScript course build
  • Custom course player
  • Offline single-file build
  • AI voice-over
  • Personal summary card

Accessibility

  • Large text with three sizes, narration at about 130 words a minute with 0.75× speed
  • Transcript on every screen, no timers and no tests to fail

At a glance

Audience
Older Australians who are new to computers or not yet confident online, learning at home or with a community program
Format
Self-paced course in a custom player that also runs offline from a single file
Structure
A welcome, 4 lessons and a wrap-up, 23 short screens, about 45 minutes
Resources
A printable summary card, plus where to get help in Australia: Scamwatch and Be Connected
Accessibility
Large text from the start with three text sizes, narration at about 130 words a minute with 0.75× speed, transcript on every screen, no timers and no tests to fail

Highlights

  • Animation

    A gently ringing call animation in the video-call practice, slow and calm by design.

  • Design system

    Built with my SS Learning Design system (AI-assisted, created with Claude): its type and colour tokens, scaled up with three text sizes for older learners.
