Active Supervision: Safeguarding the Start

Sector & audienceEarly childhood education and care · graduate educators in their first 30 days, counted in the 1:4 toddler ratio

Target behaviour changeStand where every child stays in sight during room transitions, instead of clustering at activities or stepping out of the room.

Ecosystem deliverables
  • 30-day phased induction pathway
  • Four-lesson course with the Wattle Room Sandbox simulation
  • Active Supervision transition job aid
  • Buddy shifts alongside the course

My roleLearning experience designer and developer: supervision risk analysis, objectives, scenario writing, line-of-sight simulation, build, job aid, accessibility and functional testing.

Start the courseAll case studies

Engaging learning experience

  • Three rounds that unlock one after another: morning play, the transition to outdoor play and pick-up time
  • Live “In sight” and “In the room” counters, plus a score after every 60-second run: the percentage of child-time in sight
  • Unlimited retries, with a “Show me a strong position” hint after two tries
  • A reward for mastery: the job aid unlocks only when you hold the room in all three rounds and pass the compulsory Quiz

Technical stack

  • Custom HTML5, CSS and JavaScript course build
  • SCORM 2004 / xAPI tracking
  • Line-of-sight engine: sight lines traced against walls and the cubby house
  • Wattle Room floor plan with live view cones
  • Printable Active Supervision job aid

Accessibility

  • Spots are chosen with radio buttons, so no dragging is needed, and every change on the floor plan is also described in text
  • Accessibility and functional testing as part of the build
  • Automated WCAG 2.2 AA check (axe-core) on the opening screen: no issues found

At a glance

Audience
Graduate early childhood educators
Format
Custom HTML5 course with a supervision sandbox simulation and a compulsory branching Quiz, SCORM-ready
Structure
4 lessons: Context, Challenge, Activity, Results
Standards
NQS 2.2.1, 2.2.3 · National Law s.165, s.169 · Regs 99, 122, 123 · Child Safe Principles 5 & 8
Tracking
SCORM 2004 / xAPI

Highlights

  • Animation

    Children move around the floor plan for 60 seconds while sight lines update live, then a replay slider jumps to the moment the fewest were in sight. Reduced motion skips straight to the result.

  • Design system

    Built with my SS Learning Design system (AI-assisted, created with Claude): Inter and Lato type, colour tokens and the shared course template, with the sandbox plugged in as a reusable simulation block.
