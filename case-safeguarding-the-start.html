Safeguarding the Start

A 30-day phased induction that gets graduate educators ready for their first day counted in ratio, built around a branching transition scenario in a toddler room.

Challenge & context

Designed a 30-day phased induction for new graduate educators to master active supervision, 1:4 toddler ratio compliance and threshold safety during high-stress room transitions.

Instructional strategy

Blended learning: short context lessons, a branching scenario simulation and a printable job aid, designed to sit alongside on-shift buddy mentoring across the onboarding milestones.

Content & compliance

Built on National Quality Standard Element 2.2.1 (Supervision), the Education and Care Services National Law and the National Principles for Child Safe Organisations.

Interactive features

Hotspot risk scan of the room, then a branching Test Me where every choice plays out in the room. Live "In sight" and "Ratio" counters, consequence layers and a retry from the start of the transition.

SG-L1-01Lesson opener

Day 14: counted in ratio

On screen
Transitions are the riskiest minutes in a toddler room. Today is the first day you’re counted in ratio.
Interaction
Role card (Graduate Educator, Wattle Room, 18–24 months) and three stat tiles: 8 toddlers, 1:4 ratio, 2 zones.
Audio
Narrator reads the opener. Captions on.
SG-L1-02Timeline
Timeline

Your phased induction

On screen
Days 1–5 shadowing (supernumerary) · Days 6–13 supported practice · Day 14 counted in ratio · Weeks 3–12 consolidation.
Interaction
Static four-step timeline. Day 14 is highlighted as "today".
Audio
No narration. Reading pace.
SG-L1-03Accordion
Accordion

What applies here

On screen
NQS Element 2.2.1 · National Law s.165 and Regulation 123 · National Principles for Child Safe Organisations · Active supervision: line of sight and touch.
Interaction
Four panels. The last panel explains where to stand: back to the wall, facing the room, at the threshold during transitions.
Audio
None.
SG-L2-01Hotspot

Two demands, one doorway

On screen
11:30 AM. Oliver is distressed at the main door latch. Sam asks you to step into the kitchen for the meal trays. Before you act, read the room.
Interaction
Room floor plan with four numbered markers: the door latch, the co-educator on the bottle log, the open doorway to the playground prep area, and the kitchen door (1:4 becomes 1:8 if you leave).
Audio
Room ambience, low volume.
SG-L3-01Branching scenario

Test Me: hold the line of sight

On screen
There are 8 toddlers and 2 educators. Sam is filling in the bottle log. Three children are putting on hats outside.
Interaction
Tracked variables: Children_In_Sight, Ratio_Compliant, Zone_Coverage, Supervision_Choice, Attempts. "In sight" and "Ratio" counters update with every choice.
Audio
Scene audio. The noise meter and captions show the same information for learners without sound.
SG-L3-02Feedback layer
Feedback layer

Consequence and retry

On screen
A wrong choice plays out in the room: a child leaves the learner’s line of sight or the room drops out of ratio.
Interaction
Consequence layer, explanation, then "Retry from the start of the transition". xAPI records each attempt and interaction.
Audio
Short alert tone on a ratio breach.

Interaction
A4 job aid card. Locked until the scenario is passed, then opens to print or save.

None.
