Safeguarding the Start

A 30-day phased induction that gets graduate educators ready for their first day counted in ratio, built around a branching transition scenario in a toddler room.

My role

What I did on this project

Workplace project, built for an organisation’s internal staff.

RoleLearning designer & developer
Project typeWorkplace project
What I didAnalysis of the supervision risk, learning objectives, scenario writing, room floor plan and branching logic, course build, job aid design, accessibility and functional testing
ToolsHTML, CSS and JavaScript course template · SCORM 2004 / xAPI
TeamSolo, end to end. Course built for the organisation’s internal staff.

Challenge & context

Designed a 30-day phased induction for new graduate educators to master active supervision, 1:4 toddler ratio compliance and threshold safety during high-stress room transitions.

Instructional strategy

Blended learning: short context lessons, a branching scenario simulation and a printable job aid, designed to sit alongside on-shift buddy mentoring across the onboarding milestones.

Content & compliance

Built on National Quality Standard Element 2.2.1 (Supervision), the Education and Care Services National Law and the National Principles for Child Safe Organisations.

Interactive features

Hotspot risk scan of the room, then a branching Test Me where every choice plays out in the room. Live "In sight" and "Ratio" counters, consequence layers and a retry from the start of the transition.
